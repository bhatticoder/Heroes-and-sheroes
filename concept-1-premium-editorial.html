Editor’s Note · 03
From the Editor’s Desk

Why We Give Honor

Heroes & Sheros Magazine is built around one simple idea: people who make a difference deserve to be seen, heard, and honored. In every issue we shine a light on firefighters and teachers, officers and veterans, foster families and caregivers, researchers and volunteers — and on the organizations, companies and communities that stand behind them.

Our motto, “Giving Honor Where Honor Is Due,” is more than a tagline. It is a promise to report with care, to verify before we publish, and to celebrate generosity wherever we find it — from a national foundation serving the most vulnerable to a neighbor quietly changing one street at a time. We are also proud to announce that the Heroic Hearts Podcast is coming soon, carrying these conversations beyond the printed page with the permission and participation of the people we honor.

This premiere issue is just the beginning. A paper edition arrives in January 2027, and every story you send us helps shape what comes next. If you know someone with a heroic heart, tell us about them. It would be our honor to tell their story.

“People who make a difference deserve to be seen, heard, and honored.”
Paula Skipper PeeplesChief Editor · Heroes & Sheros Magazine

Tim Tebow Foundation: Serving the Most Vulnerable

Founded in 2010, the foundation describes its mission as bringing faith, hope and love to people facing some of the darkest times in their lives — with a special focus on the “Most Vulnerable People” around the world.

2010Year founded
3-PartPrevention · Rescue · Survivor Care
GlobalPartners in the U.S. and internationally

One of the foundation’s major areas of work is the fight against human trafficking and child exploitation. Its approach has three parts: prevention through education and training; rescue through partnerships that support rescue missions and immediate survivor needs; and survivor care focused on longer-term recovery and support.

Its survivor-care work includes communities of care and safe homes designed to help people rebuild their lives with dignity. The foundation’s current website describes ongoing work with partners in the United States and internationally.

Heroes & Sheros Magazine is featuring the Tim Tebow Foundation as a heroic organization because its work aligns with our focus on recognizing organizations that serve vulnerable people and communities — quietly, consistently, and with honor.

T.I. & Tiny Harris: Community, Family and Affordable Housing

An Atlanta music legend and a Grammy-winning singer-songwriter — and a ribbon-cutting that turned a development into 143 new homes.

Intrada Westside — Fact File

  • Location — Atlanta, Georgia
  • Opened — November 2023
  • Homes — 143 residential units, 100% affordable
  • Supportive housing — 25 permanent supportive units
  • Ribbon-cutting — T.I. joined Atlanta Mayor Andre Dickens and project partners

T.I., also known as Clifford Joseph Harris Jr., is an Atlanta rapper, actor and entrepreneur. Tameka “Tiny” Harris is a singer and songwriter who rose to prominence as a member of Xscape and is also known for her songwriting. Together, they have built a public record of community giving — and one documented example stands out.

Atlanta Housing reported that Intrada Westside opened in November 2023 as a 100-percent affordable housing community with 143 residential units, including 25 units designated for permanent supportive housing. T.I. participated in the ribbon-cutting ceremony alongside Atlanta Mayor Andre Dickens and other project partners.

The project gives Heroes & Sheros a concrete community-impact story to highlight: an entertainment figure investing presence, not just publicity, in the neighborhood that shaped him — and a family name attached to real keys, real doors and real neighbors.

“Giving honor where honor is due — some honors are built, not awarded.”
— Editorial framing, Heroes & Sheros Magazine

The Interview · Verified Reporting

Fostering the Future: Expanding Opportunity for Foster Youth

Documented actions, clear sourcing and no political endorsement — our editorial standard for every story we publish.

Current official White House materials describe the Fostering the Future initiative and related actions. In November 2025, the White House announced an Executive Order titled “Fostering the Future for American Children and Families,” describing an initiative led by First Lady Melania Trump focused on expanding educational, employment and mentorship opportunities for youth transitioning from foster care to adulthood.

In June 2026, the White House announced the launch of Fostering the Future Accounts, describing them as a savings and investment vehicle for youth in foster care. The announcement said the accounts were developed with the U.S. Department of the Treasury and that 23 governors had pledged to establish accounts in their states.

For Heroes & Sheros readers, the throughline is simple: a growing national conversation about what happens to young people the moment foster care ends. Our report sticks to the documented record — the order, the accounts, the pledges — and leaves the politics to others.
