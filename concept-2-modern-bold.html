SERVING THE
MOST VULNERABLE

The Tim Tebow Foundation, founded in 2010, describes its mission as bringing faith, hope and love to people facing some of the darkest times in their lives — with a focus on the “Most Vulnerable People” around the world.

Founded 2010PreventionRescueSurvivor CareSafe HomesGlobal Partners

One of the foundation’s major areas of work is the fight against human trafficking and child exploitation. Its approach comes in three parts: prevention through education and training; rescue through partnerships supporting rescue missions and immediate survivor needs; and survivor care focused on longer-term recovery.

Its survivor-care work includes communities of care and safe homes designed to help people rebuild their lives with dignity — a model built around belonging, not just shelter. The foundation works with partners in the United States and internationally.

Heroes & Sheros features the Tim Tebow Foundation as a heroic organization because its work matches our mission: honoring the people and organizations that serve vulnerable communities, quietly and consistently.

Every claim in this feature is checked against the foundation’s official information before publication — our standard for every story in every issue.

T.I. & TINY HARRIS
BUILDING ATLANTA’S FUTURE

T.I., also known as Clifford Joseph Harris Jr., is an Atlanta rapper, actor and entrepreneur. Tameka “Tiny” Harris is a singer and songwriter who rose to prominence as a member of Xscape. One documented example of their community work: Intrada Westside.

Atlanta Housing reported the development opened in November 2023 as a 100-percent affordable community — 143 residential units, including 25 for permanent supportive housing. T.I. joined the ribbon-cutting with Mayor Andre Dickens and project partners.

FOSTERING THE FUTURE

Documented actions, clear sourcing, no endorsement — how a growing national conversation is changing what happens when foster care ends.

NOVEMBER 2025 — The White House announced an Executive Order titled “Fostering the Future for American Children and Families,” describing an initiative led by First Lady Melania Trump focused on expanding educational, employment and mentorship opportunities for youth transitioning from foster care to adulthood.

JUNE 2026 — The White House announced the launch of Fostering the Future Accounts — described as a savings and investment vehicle for youth in foster care, developed with the U.S. Department of the Treasury. According to the announcement, 23 governors pledged to establish accounts in their states.

WHY IT MATTERS — For Heroes & Sheros readers, the throughline is simple: what happens the moment foster care ends? This report sticks to the documented record — the order, the accounts, the pledges — and leaves the politics to others.

OUR STANDARD — Stories about current political figures are published as factual reporting with clear sourcing and without political endorsement. Names, dates and claims are verified before publication; links are checked at press time.
