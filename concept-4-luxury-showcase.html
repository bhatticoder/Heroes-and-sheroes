Editor's Note | 03
A letter from the Chief Editor

Why we give honor

Heroes & Sheros Magazine is built around one simple idea: people who make a difference deserve to be seen, heard, and honored.

In every issue we shine a light on individuals, organizations, families, professionals and everyday people whose actions show compassion, courage, service or perseverance.

Our motto, "Giving Honor Where Honor Is Due," is more than a tagline. It is a promise to report with care, to verify before we publish, and to celebrate generosity wherever we find it, from the firehouse to the front porch.

This premiere issue covers the work of the Tim Tebow Foundation against human trafficking, the opening of Intrada Westside in Atlanta, and the national conversation around Fostering the Future. Each story was checked against official sources before it reached these pages, and each one carries its source so you can check it too.

We are an online magazine now, with a paper edition planned for January 2027. Wherever you are reading, the standard does not change: real people, honest reporting, and honor given where honor is due.

Paula Skipper PeeplesChief Editor, Heroes & Sheros Magazine

    Tim Tebow Foundation | Since 2010

    Serving the most vulnerable

    Reporting from the foundation's public record | Sample feature

    Founded in 2010, the Tim Tebow Foundation describes its mission as bringing faith, hope and love to people facing some of the darkest times in their lives, with a focus on what it calls the Most Vulnerable People around the world.

    One of the foundation's major areas of work is the fight against human trafficking and child exploitation. The approach has three parts: prevention through education and training, rescue through partnerships that support rescue missions, and care for survivors as they rebuild their lives.

    "People who make a difference deserve to be seen, heard, and honored."From the Heroes & Sheros About page

    Heroes & Sheros features the organization because the work matches our mission: honoring the people and organizations that serve vulnerable communities quietly, consistently and without asking for attention.

    The full story continues with the numbers behind the programs, the partners involved, and what the foundation says comes next. Sources for every claim appear on the closing page of this feature.

    The block behind the ribbon

    A Heroes & Sheros sample feature | Facts checked, interviews pending

    In November 2023, T.I. joined Atlanta Mayor Andre Dickens and project partners for the ribbon cutting of Intrada Westside, a development that brought 143 residential units to the city's west side. All of the units are affordable housing, and 25 of them were set aside as permanent supportive housing.

    T.I., born Clifford Joseph Harris Jr., is an Atlanta rapper, actor and entrepreneur. Tameka "Tiny" Harris is a singer and songwriter who rose to prominence as a member of the group Xscape. Between them are decades of music, business and a family life lived, mostly, in the city they call home.

    "Giving honor where honor is due: some honors are built, not awarded."Heroes & Sheros editorial | Sample line

    What makes the story worth a magazine's attention is not the guest list. It is the accountability: when a public figure puts a name to a ribbon, the units opened, the rents held and the repairs made all belong to the record. This magazine intends to return to those numbers in future issues.

    Fostering the Future | Official record

    What the record shows

    Interview setup for the print edition. Layout image
    Reported from White House and Treasury releases | Sample frame

    For Heroes & Sheros readers, the throughline is simple: what happens the moment foster care ends? A young person aging out of the system faces a first apartment, a first paycheck and a first month with nobody behind them. The national conversation around Fostering the Future is, at heart, a conversation about that month.

    November 2025: the order
    The White House announced an Executive Order titled "Fostering the Future for American Children and Families," describing an initiative led by First Lady Melania Trump focused on expanding education and opportunity for youth in foster care. Sample
    June 2026: the accounts
    The White House announced the launch of Fostering the Future Accounts, described as a savings and investment vehicle for youth in foster care, developed with the U.S. Department of Treasury, with pledges from private partners.
    Our standard
    Stories about current political figures are published as factual reporting with clear sourcing and without political endorsement. Every claim on this page is tied to an official release, and corrections run in the next issue and online.
