How these are built

  • Single column, 600px wide, with system-safe fallbacks so the layout holds in Outlook, Apple Mail and Gmail.
  • The masthead repeats at the top of every edition, and the strip at the foot carries the issue line. Unsubscribe and preference links appear in every send, as the rules require.
  • Copy leads with a person or a number, never with a slogan. Claim-level sourcing stays in the article, not the email.
  • Photography is licensed stock for layout. At launch, each image is swapped for the client's permission-cleared photo and credited in the caption.
  • Send-from name, editorial address and subscription links are marked to confirm before the first send.
