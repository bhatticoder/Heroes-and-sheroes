One brand, six deliverables

Facts the copy rests on

  • Motto, mission line and About-page quote, used exactly as approved.
  • Tim Tebow Foundation: founded 2010; faith, hope and love mission line; prevent, rescue and care approach to trafficking.
  • Intrada Westside: Atlanta, opened November 2023, 143 affordable units, 25 permanent supportive, ribbon cutting with Mayor Andre Dickens.
  • Fostering the Future: Executive Order November 2025, accounts launched June 2026 with Treasury, reported without endorsement.
  • Contact address, live links and dates still open are marked to confirm instead of being invented.

Assets in this folder

  • Logo: assets/hh-logo-web.png, the client's diamond-and-cape mark on a transparent background, with the original files kept alongside.
  • Photography: 27 semantic copies in assets/img/, named by subject (cover-community, wheelchair-child, church, homes, atlanta and so on).
  • Captions credit every image and mark layout stock until the client's permission-cleared shots arrive.
  • Palette from the logo: navy #101E45, royal blue #1739C9, gold #D9A269, crimson #A3192E, crystal blue #2B6FB8, on ivory #FAF9F4.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts: the four concepts each carry their own pairing, the support pages share Playfair, Lora and Archivo.

How round two answers the review

  • Decorative dashes, middle dots and arrows cut from all visible copy; separators are plain bars, rules or spacing.
  • Layout no longer narrates itself inside the layout; page labels are short, like Cover or Contents.
  • Placeholder filler replaced with real editorial copy: every article states its sources, unverifiable anecdotes are withheld, and remaining unknowns are marked to confirm.
  • Quotes that have not been signed off carry a sample marker; the review notice sits above each file, not inside the design.
