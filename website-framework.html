Heroes & Sheros Magazine · Website Blueprint

Website Framework & Information Architecture

The site is built as a clean online magazine — not one long document. This blueprint maps every page, section and template from the client’s content master, ready to be skinned with any of the four visual directions (or a hybrid).

1 · Sitemap — the eight main pages

Navigation stays simple and visible on desktop and mobile: eight items, no nesting.

HomeHero banner → mission → featured hero → organization → stories → CTA → podcast → footer
About UsMission, motto, what we cover, editorial standard, CTA
Heroes & SherosHonoree directory — card grid by category
Heroic OrganizationsOrganization profiles — mission, initiatives, donate link
Latest StoriesArchive — 6–12 article cards, categories, read more
Submit a Hero or SheroNomination form + permission notice (main CTA)
Heroic Hearts PodcastComing-soon page → episode archive later
ContactEmail, phone, social, mailing address, form CTA
Article template (dynamic)
  • /article/<slug> pages created from one template
  • Categories: Heroes & Sheros · Heroic Organizations
    • Global chrome
  • Header: logo + 8-item nav (sticky on scroll)
  • Motto directly under logo or in hero banner
  • Footer: links · contact · social · Privacy · Terms

    3 · Article page template (used for every story)

    One reusable template — header, body modules, footer actions. Social share buttons on every article.

    A · Article header
    Large hero image → category label → headline → short deck → byline → published date (single H1 per page)
    B · Article body
    Opening paragraph · short subheadings · pull quote / highlighted fact · photo captions · official external link (verified URL) · related stories
    C · Sidebar
    Fact file · category chips · “Related honors” · share block
    D · Article footer actions
    [SHARE THIS STORY] · [SUBMIT YOUR OWN HERO STORY] · [READ MORE STORIES]
    Editorial rules baked into the template
  • Verify names, dates, numbers and claims before publishing
  • Public officials: factual, sourced, non-endorsing
  • No Word-template filler (Page XX, fake captions, fake dates)
    • Image alt text convention
  • “T.I. at Intrada Westside affordable housing ribbon cutting” — never “IMG_1234”

    • 7 · Responsive, behavior & SEO notes

    Responsive rules
  • Desktop: 12-col grid, generous white space; magazine feel
  • Tablet: cards to 2-col; hero art stays full-bleed
  • Mobile: single column; sticky header with menu button; big tap targets
  • Check mobile view before launch (release checklist)
    • SEO / page settings
  • Home: “Heroes & Sheros Magazine | Giving Honor Where Honor Is Due”
  • Submit: “Submit a Hero or Shero | Heroes & Sheros Magazine”
  • Podcast: “Heroic Hearts Podcast | Heroes & Sheros Magazine”
  • One clear H1 per page · descriptive image alt text
    • Design direction (from the content master)
  • Polished, warm, uplifting and editorial — not a generic template
  • Logo prominent; motto directly beneath it
  • Large high-quality photos, short readable paragraphs
  • Consistent article cards + category labels
  • One strong CTA color throughout
  • Simple visible nav; generous white space

    • 8 · Pre-launch checklist

    Content
    Replace every [ADD EMAIL] / [ADD PHONE] / [ADD SOCIAL] / [ADD PODCAST LINK] placeholder · upload final logo + consistent header treatment · correct hero photo per article · no Word-template filler
    Structure
    Create the eight main navigation pages · article pages from the template · categories for Heroes & Sheros and Heroic Organizations · turn all “CLICK HERE” text into real buttons
    Trust & legal
    Verify every external link · get photo/publication permissions where required · fact-check names, dates, numbers, claims · political stories stay factual and sourced · Privacy Policy + Terms · clear editorial email
    QA
    Test every form and button · check mobile view · add search once the archive grows · create a backup/export before major changes
