The Interview

T.I. & Tiny Harris, in conversation

An Atlanta rapper, actor and entrepreneur. A singer and songwriter who rose to fame with Xscape. A marriage, a family — and a ribbon-cutting that matters more than any trophy.

“What does community mean to you — not the word, the work?”

The record shows it clearly: T.I., also known as Clifford Joseph Harris Jr., put his name and presence behind Intrada Westside in Atlanta — not just at the podium, but on the ground with the people who built it.

“And the numbers behind it?”

Atlanta Housing reported the community opened in November 2023 as 100-percent affordable: 143 residential units, including 25 designated for permanent supportive housing. T.I. joined the ribbon-cutting alongside Mayor Andre Dickens and project partners.

“Why does Heroes & Sheros tell this story?”

Because “Giving Honor Where Honor Is Due” means honoring what is built, not only what is announced. Real walls, real keys, real neighbors.

“One line for the readers?”

“Some honors are built, not awarded.”

The Story Behind the Story

Intrada Westside, from the ground up

Every community has a birthday. Intrada Westside’s is November 2023 — the month it opened as a 100-percent affordable housing community in Atlanta: 143 residential units, twenty-five of them set aside for permanent supportive housing, and a ribbon cut by people who believed the street could hold more stories than it had before.

This is the biography of a block: not a company profile, but a story about what happens after the ceremony — the porches, the mailboxes, the neighbors learning each other’s names.

BEFORE 2023 · THE IDEA

Affordable housing conversations move from policy papers to blueprints — with community partners at the table.

NOVEMBER 2023 · THE OPENING

Intrada Westside opens: 143 homes, 100% affordable, 25 permanent supportive units. T.I. joins Mayor Andre Dickens and project partners for the ribbon-cutting.

TODAY · THE NEIGHBORHOOD

Residents, caregivers and community organizations continue the work the ribbon only began.

NEXT · EVERY ISSUE

Heroes & Sheros keeps following the story — with updates, photos and reader nominations.

Serving the Most Vulnerable, with faith, hope and love

The Tim Tebow Foundation was founded in 2010. Its mission statement is simple to say and hard to live: bring faith, hope and love to people facing some of the darkest times in their lives.

01
Prevention

Education and training that stop exploitation before it starts.

02
Rescue

Partnerships supporting rescue missions and immediate survivor needs.

03
Survivor Care

Communities of care and safe homes — longer-term recovery, with dignity.

Heroes & Sheros features the Tim Tebow Foundation as a heroic organization because its work matches our mission — honoring the people and organizations that serve vulnerable communities.
